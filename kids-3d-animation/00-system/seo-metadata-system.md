# Titles, Descriptions & the Idea Bank

---

## Title formulas

Made-for-kids search is done by **parents typing into a phone one-handed**, and by
autoplay. Write for both: front-load the searchable concept, keep the character name in
for brand recall, stay under ~60 characters so nothing truncates.

### Slot A — Poem / Rhyme

```
<Concept> | <Hook Phrase> | Puddleberry Hollow Rhymes
```

- `Five Sleepy Fireflies | Bedtime Counting Song | Puddleberry Hollow`
- `Rainy Day Puddle Song | Nursery Rhyme for Toddlers`

Lead with the **searchable noun** — counting, bedtime, colours, ABC, animals. Those are
the actual queries.

### Slot B — Story

```
<Story Title> | A Puddleberry Hollow Story for Kids
```

- `The Lantern That Wouldn't Light | A Bedtime Story for Kids`

No keyword stuffing here. The story slot is your prestige slot; a clean title signals
quality and quality is what gets it recommended.

### Slot C — Short

```
<Punchy 2-5 words> 😂 #shorts
```

- `Zip Gets the Hiccups 😂 #shorts`
- `Don't Sneeze, Zip! #shorts`

One emoji max. The `#shorts` tag in the title is still worth including.

**Never do this:**
`Kids Song For Kids Children Baby Toddler Nursery Rhyme Learning Video Fun 3D Cartoon`
— keyword chains are a documented low-quality signal and they read as spam to parents.

## Description template

```
<One-sentence hook that reads like a parent describing it to another parent.>

🌙 About this video
<2–3 sentences. What happens, and what the child gets out of it. Name the skill if
there is one — counting, colours, sharing, asking for help.>

👶 Best for ages <range>
⏱️ Runtime <mm:ss>

📖 What happens
0:00 <Beat>
0:xx <Beat>
0:xx <Beat>

🎵 Sing along
<Chorus lyrics, plain text — this is what actually pulls search traffic on rhyme videos,
because parents search half-remembered lyrics.>

✨ More from Puddleberry Hollow
Bedtime Rhymes → <playlist link>
Story Time → <playlist link>
Momo & Bibi Shorts → <playlist link>

🎨 Made with love in Puddleberry Hollow — a gentle place with no sharp edges.
This video is made for children. Original music and original characters.
Contains AI-assisted animation.

#PuddleberryHollow #KidsSongs #NurseryRhymes #BedtimeStories #3DAnimation
```

Points that actually matter:

- **First 150 characters** are what shows in search results. Front-load them.
- **Chapters/timestamps** genuinely help watch time on the 5-minute story.
- **Full chorus lyrics in the description** is the single highest-ROI SEO move in kids
  music. Parents search lyrics constantly.
- 3–5 hashtags. More gets ignored.
- Keep the AI disclosure line — it's honest, it's increasingly required, and parents in
  this category are actively checking.

## Tags (still worth 30 seconds)

Layer three types:

```
BROAD     kids songs, nursery rhymes, bedtime stories, kids cartoon, 3d animation
SPECIFIC  counting song for toddlers, firefly song, bedtime counting rhyme
BRAND     puddleberry hollow, momo and bibi, momo the fox
```

Roughly 10–15 tags. Brand tags matter more than you'd think — they're how your own
videos become each other's suggested-next.

## Upload cadence

Post at the **same times every day**. Parents build routines and the algorithm rewards
predictability.

| Slot | Suggested time (local to your biggest audience) | Why |
|---|---|---|
| C — Short | 07:00 | Morning phone scroll, breakfast |
| A — Poem | 17:00 | Pre-dinner wind-down |
| B — Story | 19:00 | Bedtime. This is the big one. |

Schedule the whole week on Sunday if you can — it decouples "did I ship" from "did I
have a bad day".
