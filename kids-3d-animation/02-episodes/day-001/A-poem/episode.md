# A — "Five Sleepy Fireflies"

**Slot:** Poem / bedtime counting rhyme
**Style:** Plush Hollow (needle-felted, macro, golden→indigo)
**Runtime:** 2:35 · **Aspect:** 16:9 · **Ages:** 1–4
**Cast:** Momo, Nana Moon (Bibi cameo)

---

## Why this one is first

It stacks the two highest-performing preschool forms in a single video: a **countdown**
(5→1) and a **bedtime ritual**. Countdowns give a toddler something to predict, and
prediction is what produces replays. The bedtime frame gives the parent a reason to make
it a nightly habit — which is the only kind of audience that compounds.

It also introduces Momo and Nana Moon with zero exposition. Nobody explains who they
are; they're just there, being gentle. That's how you launch a world.

---

## Lyrics

```
[Intro — spoken, very soft]
Hush, hush, Puddleberry.
The lanterns are low.

[Verse 1]
Five sleepy fireflies,
Blinking in the blue.
One found a daisy cup
And curled up in the dew.

[Chorus]
Blink, blink, little light,
Blink, blink, goodnight.
Blink, blink, little light,
Sleep till morning bright.

[Verse 2]
Four sleepy fireflies,
Drifting past the stream.
One rode a lily pad
And floated into dream.

[Chorus]

[Verse 3]
Three sleepy fireflies,
Wobbling round the tree.
One hid beneath a leaf,
As quiet as can be.

[Verse 4]
Two sleepy fireflies,
Yawning in the air.
One landed on Momo's ear
And fell asleep right there.

[Chorus — quieter]

[Verse 5]
One sleepy firefly,
The very last to glow.
She looked up at the moon
And whispered, soft and low...

[Bridge — humming, Nana Moon]
Mmm... mmm... goodnight, goodnight.
Mmm... mmm... sleep tight, sleep tight.

[Outro — spoken over humming, fading]
No more fireflies.
The hollow's gone to sleep.
Close your eyes now, little one,
And count them in your dreams.

Five... four... three... two... one.

[fade out]
```

**Craft notes:** every line is 5–7 syllables. Every rhyme lands on a concrete noun
(`dew`, `dream`, `be`, `there`, `low`). The chorus repeats four times unchanged — that is
the product, not laziness. The final spoken countdown gives the child something to do
with their mouth as they fall asleep.

## Production notes

- **Colour journey:** shot 01 is warm golden dusk, shot 20 is deep indigo. Grade the
  sequence as one continuous sunset. Every still prompt must state its time of day.
- **The glow is the only saturated thing in the film.** Everything else stays pastel and
  desaturated so the fireflies read as light sources.
- **No hard cuts.** Cross-dissolve 12–16 frames between every shot. On a bedtime video
  hard cuts are little adrenaline hits and they defeat the purpose.
- **Volume envelope:** peak at the second chorus, then descend continuously to the end.
  The last 30 seconds should be measurably quieter than the first 30.
- **Never show a firefly "going out."** They dim and are hidden. Nothing in this video
  disappears — a 2-year-old reads extinguishing as loss.
