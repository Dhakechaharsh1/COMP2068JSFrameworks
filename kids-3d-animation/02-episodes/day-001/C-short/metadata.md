## What to watch in analytics

Shorts have their own metrics and they mean different things than long-form.

| Metric | Target | What a miss means |
|---|---|---|
| **Viewed vs. swiped away** | > 70% | Your first 2 seconds failed. The hiccup must land before 0:02. |
| **Average view duration** | > 30s of 35s | Find the frame where the graph drops — that gag is too slow. |
| **Watched full video** | > 55% | Under 40%: cut 5 seconds. Shorts are always too long. |
| **Loops / replays** | any is good | This is why there's no end card. |

**The single highest-leverage fix on any underperforming Short is cutting the first
second.** If the swipe-away rate is bad, delete shot 01 entirely and open cold on the
hiccup. The wind-up is nice, it isn't necessary, and 1.2 seconds is a lot at this length.

## Series potential

If this performs, "X Gets the Hiccups" is a format, not a one-off:

- *Momo Gets the Hiccups* — he tries to hide them during story time
- *Gogo Gets the Hiccups* — one hiccup, thirty seconds apart, twice. That's the whole video.
- *Everyone Gets the Hiccups* — the full-cast payoff

**Gogo's is the best of the three.** An anti-comedy Short on a channel of loud ones will
outperform, and it's four shots of work.

Rotate the backdrop colours on each so the Shorts feed doesn't look repetitive — that's
variation of execution, which reads as a series rather than as reuploaded content.
