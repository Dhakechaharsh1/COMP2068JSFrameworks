# C — "Zip Gets the Hiccups"

**Slot:** Short
**Style:** Pop Vinyl (glossy toy 3D)
**Runtime:** 0:35 · **Aspect:** 9:16 vertical · **Ages:** 3–8 + parents
**Cast:** Zip, Bibi, Momo
**Dialogue:** none

---

## Why no dialogue

Three reasons, and they're all commercial:

1. **It works muted.** A large share of Shorts get watched with sound off.
2. **It travels.** No dialogue means no localization, so the same file works in every
   market. For a channel trying to grow, this is the cheapest reach you will ever buy.
3. **It's faster.** No voice recording, no lip-sync, no timing dialogue to picture. This
   Short is a two-hour job, which is what makes 3-a-day sustainable.

The whole thing is carried by physical comedy and a funk track with stop-hits on the
hiccups.

---

## Structure

Rule of three, then a reversal, then a tag that loops back to the top.

| TC | Beat | Function |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | **HICCUP.** Zip launches backward out of frame. SLAM off-screen. | hook, 2 seconds |
| 0:03 | Bibi looks up, deeply unimpressed. Zip wobbles back into frame. | setup |
| 0:08 | **Remedy 1 — water.** He drinks. HICCUP. Sprays it everywhere. | try 1 |
| 0:14 | **Remedy 2 — pat on the back.** HICCUP. He rockets straight up out of frame. Empty frame. Two seconds. He drops back down. | try 2 |
| 0:20 | **Remedy 3 — Momo sneaks up to scare him.** Zip hiccups first and headbutts Momo in the chin. | try 3 / reversal |
| 0:26 | Momo now has hiccups too. Both bouncing in sync. Bibi puts a paw over her face. | escalation |
| 0:31 | Bibi hiccups. Her glasses fly off. Freeze. | tag |
| 0:34 | Cut. | loop point |

**The empty frame at 0:16 is the best joke in the video.** Two full seconds of nothing but
a coloured backdrop while Zip is somewhere above the top of the screen. Hold it longer
than feels comfortable. Do not cut away.

## The loop

The last frame (all three mid-hiccup) and the first frame (Zip calm, pre-hiccup) are
built to cut together. When it auto-replays, it reads as an endless hiccup epidemic,
which is funnier the second time and is worth measurable extra watch time.

**Do not put an end card on it.** Nothing kills a Shorts loop faster than three seconds
of branding.

## Cover frame

Shorts pull the cover from the video itself. Build the thumbnail moment **into** the
video at **0:11** — shot 07, the water spray, mid-arc, Zip's eyes at maximum bulge. Select
that as the cover in the upload flow.

## Animation notes

This style lives or dies on **squash and stretch**. Every hiccup is the same four-beat
figure:

1. **Anticipation** — 3 frames. Zip compresses down and gets wider.
2. **Snap** — 2 frames. Full extension, eyes maximum bulge, body stretched.
3. **Overshoot** — 4 frames. Past the resting pose, wobbling.
4. **Settle** — 6 frames. Back to neutral with one small residual bounce.

Sell it with **held poses**, not smooth motion. Snap into a shape, hold for 4–6 frames,
snap to the next. Smooth easing looks expensive and reads as boring at this length.

At 30 fps the whole hiccup is half a second. Time every one to a downbeat.
