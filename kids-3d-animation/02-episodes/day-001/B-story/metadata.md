## Watch-time notes

This is the video your channel gets judged on, so watch its retention graph specifically.

- **Target:** 50–70% average percentage viewed for a sub-5-minute kids video.
- **The 0:15 mark** is where you'll lose people if the cold open isn't working. If the

- **The 2:30 mark** (Act 3, where the music stops) is the risky one. Some drop-off is
