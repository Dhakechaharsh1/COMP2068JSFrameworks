## Animation

Every impact is the same four-beat figure at 30 fps:

1. **Anticipation** — 3 frames, compress and widen
2. **Snap** — 2 frames, full extension, eyes maximum
3. **Overshoot** — 4 frames, past the resting pose
4. **Settle** — 6 frames, one residual bounce

Sell it with **held poses**, not smooth motion. Snap, hold 4–6 frames, snap. Smooth easing
reads as boring at this length.

Time every impact to a downbeat.

## Sound

- Music: Suno funk loop **with stop-hits**. A groove with no rests is unusable.
- Each impact is a three-part stack: intake gasp → *boing* **on the downbeat** → result SFX.
- The impacts are the loudest thing in the video.
- **Put real silence around the two biggest jokes.** Comedy is contrast.
- Mix on a phone speaker, not headphones. Target −13 LUFS, HPF the bed at 80 Hz.
