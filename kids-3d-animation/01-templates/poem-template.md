## Production notes

- **Colour journey:** _from ______ to ______._ State the time of day in every prompt.
- **Cross-dissolve 12–16 frames between every shot.** No hard cuts on a bedtime video.
- **Volume envelope:** peak at the second chorus, descend continuously to the end.
- Bookend: the final wide should mirror the opening wide from the same camera position.
