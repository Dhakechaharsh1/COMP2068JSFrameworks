## The shape

Every story in this slot runs the same five-act skeleton. It works because the flaw
drives it — Momo *tries harder instead of asking* — and because the lesson always arrives
as somebody else's story rather than as instruction.

| Act | TC | Job |
|---|---|---|
| **Cold open** | 0:00–0:18 | **The problem is on screen inside 15 seconds.** No scene-setting. |
| **1 — The world** | 0:18–1:10 | What everyone else has that our character doesn't |
| **2 — Trying** | 1:10–2:30 | A montage of escalating solo effort. All of it fails. |
| **3 — Hiding** | 2:30–3:25 | Embarrassment. Withdrawal. **Cut the music entirely here.** |
| **4 — The elder** | 3:25–4:35 | Gogo tells a fact about himself and asks one question |
| **5 — The turn** | 4:35–5:20 | The character acts on it. Payoff. Warm ending. |

## Craft check

- [ ] Problem visible before 0:15
- [ ] The elder **never states the moral** — one fact, one question, that's all
- [ ] The child works it out; no character explains the lesson
- [ ] The payoff hesitates once before it lands
- [ ] One line written for the parent in the room
- [ ] Conflict resolves. Nobody ends the video sad or excluded.
- [ ] ≤ 3 speaking characters
- [ ] The flaw is not "fixed" — it's worked around, once
